# Licences

Checked against the linked pages on 2026-10-31. This is a reading of those pages for an engineering choice. It is not legal advice. "Commercial use" means the licence text does not forbid it. It does not mean a lawyer has cleared a product.

The repository itself is Apache-2.0. See the root `LICENSE`.

## What is committed

No dataset archives, no video frames, no `.pt` / `.pth` checkpoints. `docs/demo.gif` and `docs/trajectory.png` are rendered outputs of the demo (under 5 MB for the GIF). They are illustrations of the run, not a redistribution of the DUT archive. If you republish them, keep the DUT attribution next to them: Zhao, Zhang, Li and Wang, DUT Anti-UAV, Apache-2.0 sources linked above.
